## Mechanist: AI as a Scientific Instrument for Discovering the Mechanisms of Intelligence

[Read the paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12036)

This paper introduces an AI research system that generates hypotheses, runs experiments, and tests causal interventions to investigate the internal mechanisms of AI models and use its findings to improve their behavior.

## Thinking Inertia: LLMs Keep Thinking When Told Not To

[Read the paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11765)

This paper shows that LLMs can still produce explicit reasoning when instructed to answer directly, and that suppressing this reasoning can reduce accuracy on open-ended tasks.
